VISTA / DefectVICL

Data-efficient vision in-context learning for wafer defect classification

A vision-language framework that dynamically selects balanced examples and uses structured reasoning to classify nine wafer-map patterns. The published study reports 96% average accuracy using 0.17% of the labeled WM-811K data.

I build trustworthy AI systems for designing, manufacturing, testing, repairing, and securing semiconductor systems.

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and a Graduate Research Associate at Arizona State University. My work connects trustworthy language-model agents, electronic design automation, fab-aware defect intelligence, and machine-learning-assisted reliability, repair, and test.

My research also explores secure federated learning for advanced-packaging inspection, enabling collaborative model development without sharing sensitive manufacturing defect data while grounding each diagnosis in traceable physical, process, and test evidence.

I conduct my PhD research under the supervision of Prof. Krishnendu Chakrabarty.ASU Profile ↗

Biography

A computer scientist working closer to the chip.

My research journey began at BRAC University, where I worked on computer vision, edge AI, and secure federated learning. At ASU, that foundation evolved toward trustworthy LLMs for EDA and test, semiconductor manufacturing inspection, advanced packaging, reliability, and repair.

I also review research, mentor undergraduate projects, and speak about generative AI. Across these roles, my aim is the same: convert an ambiguous technical problem into a system that can be tested, explained, and trusted.
